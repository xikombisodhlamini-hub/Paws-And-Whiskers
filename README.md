# 1. ORGANISATION OVERVIEW

**Organisation Name:** Paws & Whiskers Animal Rescue
**Organisation Type:** Non-Profit Organisation (NPO)
**Established:** 2021

Paws & Whiskers Animal Rescue is a fictional non-profit organisation established in 2021 with the purpose of rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned, neglected, and abused animals. The organisation provides rescued animals with temporary shelter, food, veterinary care, and emotional support while working to find them safe and permanent homes.

The organisation also aims to educate communities about responsible pet ownership, animal welfare, adoption, and the importance of providing animals with safe and caring environments. Paws & Whiskers works with volunteers, foster families, veterinarians, donors, and community members to support its rescue activities.
